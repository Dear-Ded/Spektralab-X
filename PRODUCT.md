# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Android photographers, from phone users choosing a one-tap look to film enthusiasts adjusting the complete development pipeline. The same edit supports both levels of control (docs/UI2.md, section 0.1).

## Product Purpose

Develop RAW and phone photos locally, simulate film and paper, refine digital colour, and save images or reusable looks. Photos stay on the device (README.md).

## Brand Personality

Restrained, clear, photographic. The photograph has priority over interface decoration. These choices follow docs/UI2.md and the committed Theme.kt tokens.

## Anti-references

Avoid the former orange-on-black interface, colourful editor chrome, crowded controls, unexplained professional terms in the one-tap route, and unrelated visual redesigns (docs/UI2.md, sections 0, 1 and 7).

## Design Principles

- Keep one edit across quick styles and detailed controls.
- Expose common decisions first; put advanced parameters behind progressive disclosure.
- Make the applied state and the action's scope explicit.
- Preserve the photograph's neutral surround and existing component vocabulary.
- Keep preview, saved recipes and exported images consistent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use the existing inverted selected pills rather than hue alone, touch targets of at least 48 dp, Chinese labels, text of at least 12 sp, and the system's reduced-motion setting (docs/UI2.md and Theme.kt). This records existing requirements; it does not claim a completed accessibility certification.
