Spektralab-X is an Android derivative of SpektraLab (JamesQiu2005/SpektraLab) and spektrafilm: spectral film and print simulation, tuned for Xiaomi number-series phones (Snapdragon / Adreno). Unofficial; not affiliated with upstream.

It is not a filter: the photo is exposed spectrally onto a physical film model, developed into a negative and printed onto paper, all computed on the phone's GPU with Vulkan.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Camera RAW (DNG, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF and more, via LibRaw) and phone photos (JPEG, HEIC, PNG, WebP, keeping Display P3)</li>
<li>30+ colour negative, slide and black-and-white films and papers, with a live preview strip</li>
<li>Exposure, RAW white balance, print exposure, Y/M filtration, grain, halation, glare</li>
<li>Crop, rotate, flip, film edge (135, half frame, XPan, 120 formats) and date back</li>
<li>Presets, undo/redo, histogram, hold to compare, 1:1 grain view</li>
<li>Export JPEG (shooting EXIF kept, location only if you turn it on) or 16-bit TIFF</li>
</ul>

Free, no ads, no in-app purchases. Online only to check for updates; no data collection, and photos never leave the phone. Open source (GPL-3.0); the official build is free and published only on GitHub Releases.

<b>Status</b>
Alpha, under rapid development, not yet tested on real devices at scale; community feedback is needed. Built by one independent developer working with AI assistants (Claude, GPT, Grok and DeepSeek). Once the Xiaomi-tuned version is stable for daily use, separate projects are planned for a general Android version, Windows and iOS.

<i>The interface is Simplified Chinese only for now.</i>
